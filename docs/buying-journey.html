How a contractor buys from you

Seven steps, from the moment he runs out of liner to the moment he orders again. Read it left to right. The purple step is where the sale is won or lost.

MikeRuns a small lining crew. Wants supply and support, fast.
DaveNew to CIPP and growing. Wants a clear place to start.
DiscoverHe realises he needs something
EvaluateHe checks whether you have it
BuyHe asks the price and orders
KeepHe comes back
Key moment
STEP 1

Need arises

"I have a job booked and I am short on liner."

Checks what is left on the truck.

Your site

Your name is the one he remembers.

STEP 2

Search

"Who sells this, and who has it in stock?"

Searches the product name, or asks another contractor.

Your site

Google shows you, not a competitor.

STEP 3

Lands on your site

"Do they carry my size? Do they also do installs?"

Looks at the home page for about five seconds.

Your site

“We supply contractors, we do not install” in the first three seconds.

STEP 4

Checks the details

"Will this fit my gear? Where are the specs?"

Opens product pages and looks for spec sheets.

Your site

Specs and safety sheets on every product page.

STEP 5

Asks the price

"What does it cost, and can I get it this week?"

Calls you, or sends a quote request.

Your site

Short form. Name, company, phone. Your number on every screen.

STEP 6

Orders and collects

"When will it get here?"

Places the order, picks it up or takes the freight.

Your site

Clear pickup and freight information.

STEP 7

Comes back

"Who do I call when something goes wrong?"

Rings for help, books training, reorders.

Your site

Training dates, support, and an easy reorder.

Where you are losing him today

At step 3 the current site reads as though you install pipe yourself. A contractor who thinks you compete with him leaves before he ever reaches step 4, and you never hear from him. The new home page says "we supply contractors, we do not perform installations" three times before he scrolls. That one change is the biggest in the whole design.
